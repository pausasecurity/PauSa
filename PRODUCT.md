# PAUSA — Product Context

## Product Purpose
LFG (Looking For Group) platform for gamers. Players find compatible squadmates by skill, game, and play style — no Discord-scrolling, no Reddit posts. Direct squad formation.

## Register
product

## Users
Competitive and casual gamers aged 16–35 who want teammates without wasting time on bad matches. PC and console players who value speed, density, and honest interfaces. Used at night, in dim rooms, between sessions.

## Brand
- Name: PAUSA — "PAU" in Bordeaux (`#9B1631`), "SA" in Navy (`#1A2744`)
- Tagline: "find your squad" — DM Sans Italic, Gold, no punctuation before it
- Tone: direct, gaming-native, no corporate language
- Anti-references: Discord blue, Twitch purple, SaaS purple gradient, over-animated marketing sites, generic dark dashboards

## Strategic Principles
1. Density serves the user — data is the product, not the aesthetic
2. Dark-first, always — this runs at 11pm in a dim room
3. Bordeaux as single accent — functional signal, not decoration
4. Brutally honest UI — no fake engagement, no theater

## Anti-patterns (absolute bans)
- Nested cards
- Gradient text
- Inter font
- Purple or blue palette
- Hero-metric SaaS template
- Identical card grids
- Glassmorphism as default
- Emojis in UI (SVG icons only)
- Em dashes in copy
